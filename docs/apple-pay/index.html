Apple Pay Import

Each Apple Pay payment you make appears in DebtsBook under Expenses › Pending, ready to log or dismiss. You set this up once in the Shortcuts app.

Why the Shortcuts app? Apple doesn't let apps read Apple Pay payments directly. A personal automation in Shortcuts can, and only you can create it.

Payments stay on your iPhone. DebtsBook never uploads them.

  1. Open Shortcuts, go to Automation and tap +.
  2. In the Describe a shortcut box, type: When I pay with Apple Pay using any card, run the DebtsBook action "Log Apple Pay Payment" immediately.
  3. If it asks which shortcut to run, pick Log Apple Pay Payment (not "Ask Each Time").
  4. Tap Edit. The top block must read When Any Card is tapped. Remove any other step with its ✕, then search DebtsBook and add Log Apple Pay Payment under it.
  5. Link each field to the payment: tap the field, choose the Transaction variable, tap its bubble, then pick:
    • Merchant → Merchant
    • Amount → Amount
    • Card → Card or Pass
  6. Tap the › next to "is tapped": keep Any Category and Any Merchant, turn Automation on and Notify off.
  7. Tap ‹ to save. The Automation list shows When I tap a Wallet pass or payment card.
  1. Open Shortcuts, go to the Automation tab and tap + (New Automation).
  2. Choose Transaction.
  3. Select all your cards, keep every category, choose Run Immediately and tap Next.
  4. Tap New Blank Automation, search DebtsBook and add Log Apple Pay Payment.
  5. Link each field to the payment: tap the field, choose Shortcut Input, tap its bubble, then pick:
    • Merchant → Merchant
    • Amount → Amount
    • Card → Card or Pass
  6. Tap Done.

Test it

Don't use the ▶︎ button. It runs without a real payment, so you'll see Couldn't read the amount "". That's expected.

Instead, buy something small with Apple Pay, then open DebtsBook. A badge appears on Expenses and the payment is listed under Pending.

Good to know

  1. Only Apple Pay payments are captured, not physical cards or bank transfers.
  2. A payment logged twice within a minute (for example from both iPhone and Apple Watch) is only added once.
  3. Refunds come through as a positive amount. Swipe to dismiss them.
